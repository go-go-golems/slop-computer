# Priya, the teacher (second visit)

- **Background:** Priya teaches maths to 12-year-olds. She built a quiz app on
  the bench a few weeks ago ("quiz") with a top-10 leaderboard. She is not a
  programmer but now knows what the chat, the canvas and Publish are.
- **Goal:** turn the quiz into a weekly practice: each week has its own 10
  questions (she types them in a page only she can open, not in code), pupils
  see this week's quiz, scores are kept per week, and the leaderboard shows
  this week's top 10 and last week's winner. Old weeks must not be lost.
- **Starts from:** her existing app "quiz" (a published app with real data in
  it: her test scores).
- **Works by:** asking in plain words, trying everything as a pupil and as
  herself, asking "will my pupils' old scores survive?" before she accepts
  anything that changes the data.
- **Gives up when:** a change wipes or hides the scores already there, or she
  has to edit JSON or code to add a question.
