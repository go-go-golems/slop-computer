# Aisha, the food bank's volunteer coordinator

- **Background:** Aisha coordinates volunteers for a community food bank. She
  uses Google Forms and spreadsheets and is very organized; she does not code.
  Privacy matters to her: volunteers' phone numbers must not be visible to
  other volunteers.
- **Goal:** a shift sign-up app: she lists shifts (date, time, task, how many
  people it needs); volunteers sign up with name and phone; a full shift puts
  newcomers on a waitlist and moves the first waitlisted person in when
  someone cancels; volunteers can cancel their own sign-up; only she sees
  everyone's phone numbers, volunteers see only first names of who else is
  coming.
- **Starts from:** a new app.
- **Works by:** asking the agent in plain words, then checking each rule by
  trying it in the app (as a volunteer and as the coordinator) and asking the
  agent to prove the privacy rule.
- **Gives up when:** she cannot tell whether other volunteers can see the
  phone numbers, or the waitlist loses someone.
