# Priya, the teacher

- **Background:** Priya teaches 8th-grade science. She knows some HTML from a
  course years ago, and edits Google Sites. Her class already uses a quiz app
  ("quiz") that a colleague built in the bench; students take it every week.
- **Goal:** add a leaderboard to the existing quiz app: the top 10 scores of
  the week, with first names only. It must not break the quiz for her class
  while she works on it, so she wants to try the change somewhere safe and
  only publish it when it works.
- **Starts from:** the existing app "quiz" (open it; do not create a new one).
- **Works by:** asking the agent; she has heard of "proposals" or drafts and
  asks the agent how to try changes safely. She checks the quiz still works
  after the change.
- **Gives up when:** she is afraid she broke the students' quiz and cannot
  tell how to undo it.
