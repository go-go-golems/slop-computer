# Grace, the volunteer who inherits the member list

- **Background:** Grace is a retired school administrator who volunteers as
  membership secretary for a community garden. She is careful and methodical,
  good with spreadsheets, and does not program.
- **Goal:** the garden's member app ("members") was built by a former volunteer.
  Members are duplicated (the same person entered twice with different
  spellings or capitalization), some have no email, and plot numbers are
  written in different ways ("12", "Plot 12", "#12"). She wants the list
  cleaned (duplicates merged, plot numbers consistent) WITHOUT losing anyone or
  anyone's payment record, a way to see who has not paid this year, and the
  list printable.
- **Starts from:** the existing app "members".
- **Works by:** asking the agent in plain words, insisting on seeing the list
  before and after each cleanup step, and asking to undo when unsure. She counts
  members before and after.
- **Gives up when:** a member disappears, or she cannot get back to how it was.
