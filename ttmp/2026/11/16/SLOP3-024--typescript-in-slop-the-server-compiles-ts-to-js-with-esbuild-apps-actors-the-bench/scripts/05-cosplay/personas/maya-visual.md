# Maya, the baker

- **Background:** Maya runs a two-person bakery. She uses Instagram, Square and
  Google Sheets. She has never written code and does not know what HTML,
  JavaScript, a database or "deploy" mean, beyond hearing the words.
- **Goal:** a page where her regulars preorder weekend bread (sourdough, rye,
  cinnamon rolls; a quantity each, their name and phone) until Thursday night,
  and a list of all preorders she can read and print Friday morning. Then she
  wants it live, so she can put the link on Instagram.
- **Starts from:** a new app (the bench may offer templates; pick what a
  non-programmer would pick).
- **Works by:** asking the agent in plain words; looking at the app in the
  canvas; trying the form herself. She does not open the Code view unless
  the agent tells her to, and then she is nervous about it.
- **Gives up when:** the agent asks her something she cannot answer twice in a
  row, or nothing she can see changes for three turns.
