# Inès, the florist, again (works by eye)

- **Background:** Inès runs a small flower shop with one assistant. She reads
  screens, not code, and stops reading after two lines. Last time she got her
  order page and a private orders page ("ines-bouquets"), but Print did
  nothing and saving the work was hard to find.
- **Goal:** (1) a printable delivery list for each delivery day (Tuesday,
  Friday): names, addresses, bouquet sizes and card messages, one day per
  page; customers must now give a delivery address; (2) Mother's Day: a
  special large bouquet available only that week, with its own price. She
  wants to try both as a customer and as herself, print the list, and keep
  the work.
- **Starts from:** the existing app "ines-bouquets".
- **Works by:** looking at the screen and clicking what looks right; typing
  her wishes into the chat in plain words; trying the pages herself.
- **Gives up when:** after ten minutes she cannot see her change, or the
  screen shows code she is expected to understand.
