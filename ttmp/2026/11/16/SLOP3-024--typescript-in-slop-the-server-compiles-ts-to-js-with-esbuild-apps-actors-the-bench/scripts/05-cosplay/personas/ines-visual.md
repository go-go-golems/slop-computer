# Inès, the florist (works by eye)

- **Background:** Inès runs a small flower shop with one assistant. She uses a
  phone and a laptop for email and her shop's Instagram; she has never built
  anything. She reads screens, not code, and she is in a hurry: she stops
  reading after two lines.
- **Goal:** a page where her regular customers order a weekly bouquet: pick a
  size (small, medium, large, with prices), a delivery day (Tuesday or
  Friday), a card message, their name and phone. And a page only she and her
  assistant can open that lists this week's orders by delivery day, which she
  can print. She wants to try it as a customer before she shares it.
- **Starts from:** nothing: she makes a new app called "ines-bouquets".
- **Works by:** looking at the screen and clicking what looks right; typing
  her wishes into the chat in plain words; trying the page herself.
- **Gives up when:** after ten minutes she still cannot see any page of her
  own, or the screen shows code she is expected to understand.
