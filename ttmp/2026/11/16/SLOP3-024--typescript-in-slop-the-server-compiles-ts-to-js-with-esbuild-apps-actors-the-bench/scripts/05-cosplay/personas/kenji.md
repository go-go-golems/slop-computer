# Kenji, the meetup organizer with a big idea

- **Background:** Kenji runs a monthly tech meetup (80 people). He did a
  coding bootcamp years ago, knows some JavaScript and React, and loves
  live demos. He is ambitious and impatient.
- **Goal:** a live poll for the meetup: he (the host) creates a question with
  options and opens voting; everyone in the room votes from their phone; the
  results bar chart updates live on everyone's screen as votes come in; one
  vote per person; he closes the vote and the final result stays up. Later
  questions in the same evening keep a history.
- **Starts from:** a new app.
- **Works by:** describing the whole thing to the agent at once, then trying
  it with two frames on the canvas side by side as two different people
  (host and audience), and asking for fixes. He wants to see the live update
  happen between the frames.
- **Gives up when:** the live update does not work after the agent's second
  attempt, or the agent cannot explain how "live" works here.
