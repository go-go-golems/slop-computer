# Aisha, the volunteer coordinator, again

- **Background:** Aisha coordinates volunteers for a community food bank. She
  is very organized and does not code. Her shift sign-up app ("aisha-shifts2")
  works: shifts, sign-ups, a waitlist that promotes in order, cancelling by
  phone number, and phones visible only to her.
- **Goal:** (1) a no-show record: after a shift she ticks who came; three
  no-shows and a volunteer can't sign up until she clears them; (2) a weekly
  roster she can download as a CSV file for the warehouse (names, shifts,
  no phones); (3) proof that both work: tests the bench can re-run, including
  one that checks what is in the downloaded file. Privacy stays as it is.
- **Starts from:** the existing app "aisha-shifts2".
- **Works by:** asking the agent in plain words, trying everything in the
  canvas as a volunteer and as herself, asking to see the tests run, and
  asking plainly what a test checks.
- **Gives up when:** a volunteer's phone number shows up where it shouldn't,
  or a test "passes" but she can't tell what it checked.
